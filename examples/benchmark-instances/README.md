# Published Literature Examples

This directory introduces three published environmental monitoring studies used as examples for ECMonitor. The descriptions below summarize the cited publications and their public supplementary material. They do not report ECMonitor evaluation results.

| Publication | Published study | Relevance to literature data work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Nature Geoscience* (2024) | [Ackerman Grunfeld et al., “Underestimated burden of per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances in global surface waters and groundwaters”](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41561-024-01402-8) | Collates PFAS measurements in surface water and groundwater from published sources. |
| *Science Advances* (2024) | [Zhang et al., “Exploring global oceanic persistence and ecological effects of legacy persistent organic pollutants across five decades”](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.ado5534) | Synthesizes published marine POP measurements, geographic patterns, temporal trends, and ecological implications. |
| *Environmental Science & Technology* (2024) | [Arp et al., “The Global Threat from the Irreversible Accumulation of Trifluoroacetic Acid (TFA)”](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.4c06189) | Discusses TFA sources and accumulation; its public supporting information includes monitoring data. |

The folders provide brief context for each publication and illustrative files for exploring different literature data formats. Example files are demonstrations, not a released benchmark dataset or a statement of measured ECMonitor performance. For original data, definitions, and conclusions, consult the publications and their supporting information.
